## What the 18th N-2 result actually means

Two sessions ago this question could not be started — twelve minutes, then a stop.
This time all three parts were completed correctly:

```
    Heat rate    = 860/0.28              = 3071.43 kCal/kWh    ✓
    Gas rate     = 3071.43/13000         = 0.236 kg/kWh        ✓
    WHB efficiency                       = 75.53 %             ✓
    Supplementary firing                 = 559.44 kg/hr        ✓
    Separate boiler @ 80%                = 538.46 kg/hr        ✓
    Conclusion: separate boiler is economical                  ✓
```

And notably: the separate boiler was computed at **80%, the figure the question
gives** — not the 85% the official model answer uses. The instruction to follow
the question over the model was applied without prompting.

That is a topic going from "cannot begin" to "fully correct" in one session.
