## Section I — True/False (10 × 1)

| # | Statement | Ans |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Current THD is the ratio of the RMS value of the harmonic currents to the square of the fundamental current | TRUE |
| 2 | Both rotary hearth and walking beam-type furnaces are continuous furnaces | TRUE |
| 3 | %O₂ or CO₂ in flue gas is not required to calculate boiler efficiency by the **direct** method | TRUE |
| 4 | In the **reheat** cycle, partially expanded steam extracted at various points heats the condensate and feedwater through HP/LP heaters | FALSE *(that is the **regenerative** cycle, not reheat)* |
| 5 | Oxygen and nitrogen in the fuel do not contribute to its calorific value | TRUE |
| 6 | The difference between GCV and NCV of hydrogen fuel is zero | FALSE *(hydrogen has the largest gap of any fuel)* |
| 7 | In a refrigeration plant, the higher the kW/TR the higher the COP | FALSE *(reciprocals)* |
| 8 | In an integrated steel plant, pig iron is produced from the blast furnace | TRUE |
| 9 | Copper loss in a transformer is the power consumed to sustain the core magnetic field | FALSE *(that is **iron** loss; copper loss is I²R in the windings)* |
| 10 | Head generated by a centrifugal pump is proportional to the square of the liquid density | FALSE *(head is independent of density; **pressure** is proportional to density)* |

> **Q7 is the reciprocal trap, verbatim.** Q4 and Q9 are the two definition swaps
> most worth knowing — reheat vs regenerative, and iron vs copper loss.

## Section III — Long (4 × 20)

### N-1 — Shopping mall, 120 TR chiller *(HVAC + motors + pumps)*
4500 h/yr, ₹9/kWh. Load currents and power factors for compressor, condenser pump,
chiller pump and CT fan at 415 V; chilled water 23 L/s across 4.5 °C.

```
  Power   = 1.732 × 415 × I × pf/1000 for each → total 130.5 kW
  TR      = 23 × 3600 × 1 × 4.5/3024                     = 123.2 TR
  COP     = 372 600/(87.33 × 0.92 × 860)                 = 5.4
  kW/TR   = 130.5/123.2                                  = 1.059
  Pump η  = ρgQH/1000 ÷ motor kW → chiller 58.03 %, condenser 59.58 %
  After 10 % chiller reduction + 60 % pump efficiency:
          121.18 kW → 0.984 kW/TR → 41,940 kWh/yr → ₹3,77,460/yr
```

### N-2 — Chemical plant, absorption vs centrifugal chillers ⭐ *(HVAC)*
2400 TR met by centrifugal chillers at COP 4.4; 10 MT/hr of excess 8.5 bar(g) steam
currently condensed in an air-fin cooler; propose a double-effect VAM at COP 1.2.

```
  a) air fin fan motor  = 50 × 100/(102 × 0.7 × 0.92)      = 76.12 kW
  b) centrifugal chiller = 2400/(4.4 × 0.94)×(860/3024)    = 2040.4 kW
  c) VAM heat = 2400 × 3024/1.2 = 60,48,000 kCal/h
     waste steam gives 10 000 × 479 = 47,90,000
     balance 12,58,000/479                                 = 2626.3 kg/hr
  d) Condenser duty = TR × 3024 × (1 + 1/COP)
       centrifugal: 2400 × 3024 × (1 + 1/4.4)              = 89,07,055
       absorption:  2400 × 3024 × (1 + 1/1.2)              = 1,33,05,600
       ADDITIONAL                                          = 43,98,545 kCal/h
       CW = 43,98,545/(1000 × 1 × (42 − 34))               = 549.82 m³/hr
  e) Net benefit                                           = ₹886.41 lakh/yr
```

> **⭐ This is the exact concept Mock 1's N-4(A) turned on, and BEE writes the
> general form explicitly:**
>
> ```
>     Condenser duty = TR × 3024 × (1 + 1/COP)
> ```
>
> One formula for both machines. At COP 4.4 the multiplier is 1.227; at COP 1.2 it
> is 1.833 — which is why a VAM rejects ~50% more. **Add this to Day 15 Rung 163.**
