# Exam Brief — Paper 4, 26th NCE

All facts below are taken from the official 26th NCE Prospectus (BEE / NPC,
June 2026) and from the ten past papers in `papers/`.

## Structure — stable from 2015 to 2025

| Section | Type | Count | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|
| I | True / False | 10 | 10 × 1 = 10 |
| II | Short numericals | 2 | 2 × 5 = 10 |
| III | Long numericals | 4 | 4 × 20 = **80** |

Section III carries 80% of the paper. In most years the last long question
(N-4) offers a choice — "answer any ONE of A, B, C, D".

The 25th paper (2025) gave this time guidance: **Section I & II — 10 minutes
each; Section III — 100 minutes.** That was for a 2-hour paper. Your paper is
2.5 hours, so budget roughly: Section I & II 25 minutes, Section III 120
minutes, review 5 minutes.

> ⚠️ **The duration changed.** The 25th was 2 hours; the 26th is 2.5 hours.
> Do not assume the paper is the same size. Prepare for the possibility of a
> fifth long question or longer sub-parts.

## Rules that matter

**Attempt limit — check this first.** Paper 4 allows a maximum of **4 attempts
within 6 consecutive examinations**. Papers 1, 2 and 3 allow only 3. Confirm on
your candidate dashboard how many Paper 4 attempts you have already used.

**Open book means guidebooks only.** The prospectus is explicit: *"The
candidates can refer to their supplied fourth edition guidebooks. No other
books/written materials will be allowed for reference."* No printed notes, no
photocopies, no formula sheets. Anything you want in the hall must already be
inside those books — see `reference/open-book-strategy.md`.

**Supplementary candidates use 4th-edition guidebooks.** Candidates registered
for the 21st–25th exams have already been issued them. Latest versions are
downloadable free from the candidate dashboard at nceexam.in.

**Difficulty is officially higher.** The prospectus states plainly: *"The degree
of difficulty in Paper-4 will be comparatively much higher than the other
papers."* Open book is not the easy paper.

**Toppers list excludes supplementary attempts.** Irrelevant to passing, but
worth knowing so it isn't a surprise.

## What the past papers show

Ten sittings are in `papers/` — the 16th (2015) through the 25th (2025).

- **Set A and Set B (Green / Pink) are the same questions in a different
  order.** Verified across the 16th, 18th, 19th, 24th and 25th. So there are
  ten sittings' worth of unique material, not twenty.
- **All machine-readable papers include full worked solutions.** These model
  answers show the expected method and layout — they are the most valuable
  asset in this repository.
- **The 21st and 22nd papers are scanned images** with no text layer. They can
  only be read visually, page by page.

## Topic frequency

Counted across the eight machine-readable sittings. "Sittings" = how many of the
eight featured the topic substantively.

**Near-certain — appears almost every year:**

| Topic | Sittings |
|---|---|
| Boiler efficiency & evaporation ratio | 8/8 |
| Motors, VSDs and drives | 8/8 |
| HVAC, chillers, refrigeration | 7/8 |
| Pumps and pumping systems | 7/8 |
| Heat exchangers — LMTD, effectiveness | 7/8 |
| Power plant heat rate / PAT / e-certificates | 7/8 |
| Cement — kiln, clinker, preheater | 7/8 |
| Waste heat recovery and drying | 7/8 |
| Cogeneration and turbines | 7/8 |

**Periodic:** cooling towers (5/8), furnaces and reheating (4/8), fans and
blowers (4/8), fuels and combustion stoichiometry (4/8)

**Occasional, high-value:** steel, sugar/bagasse, textile, compressed air,
financial analysis (NPV/IRR/payback), insulation, DG sets

The sector questions — cement, steel, sugar, textile — correspond to the new
4th-edition Book-4 chapters 11–15, and are appearing *more* in recent years,
not less. Older prep material does not cover them.
