set from — not third-party summaries.

They are linked, not copied into this repository: BEE holds copyright and this
repo is public. Download them to your own machine.

## A caution on edition

These are **3rd edition** chapters. Book 4 there has 11 chapters; your 4th
edition has 15, adding power plants, steel, cement, buildings and textile as
separate chapters.

The core assessment methods are unchanged, so these are sound for learning. But
**the sector chapters (11–15) are not covered here**, and recent papers lean on
them increasingly. For those, your supplied 4th-edition guidebook is the only
source — and it is the only one permitted in the hall.

## What to avoid

Scribd, Yumpu, FlipHTML5 and similar aggregators host scanned copies of these
same chapters. They are paywalled, often incomplete, sometimes wrong edition,
and offer nothing the official links above don't. Skip them.

## How lessons cite sources

Each day names the chapter behind it. Where a lesson teaches something the
guidebook states differently, the lesson says so explicitly — the guidebook wins
in the exam hall, because it is what the examiner marks against.
